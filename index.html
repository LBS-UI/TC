Your Local Tambayan

Tambayan Cawag is a casual restaurant and gathering place in Cawag, Subic, Zambales — the kind of spot where the barkada meets up for silog meals, snacks, cold drinks, and coffee. Whether you're grabbing a quick solo meal or bringing the whole crew for a family bundle, Tambayan Cawag is built to be your go-to hangout.

From hearty rice meals to pulutan for sharing, our menu covers breakfast-to-midnight cravings — cafe, grill, resto, and bar, all under one roof.
